The GraphQL Foundation is a Directed Fund hosted by the Linux Foundation. It is
not a standalone legal entity. The Directed Fund raises, budgets and spends funds
in support of the GraphQL community, including the "GraphQL OSS Project", a
Series of LF Projects, LLC, and the "GraphQL Specification", a Series of JDF
Projects, LLC.

The OSS Project, together with the Specification Project, forms the Technical
Project.

You can read more about the Directed Fund, OSS Project and general structure in
the
[Foundation Participation Agreement](https://graphql.org/files/GraphQL_Foundation-Participation_Agreement-Preview.pdf)
while the
[Specification Membership Agreement](https://github.com/graphql/graphql-wg/blob/main/membership/GraphQL-Specification_Membership-2026-05-15-PREVIEW.pdf)
details the Specification Project.

## Rough mental model

A simple (but slightly inaccurate) mental model for the GraphQL organization is
that the TSC oversees all technical work (the specifications, working groups,
reference implementation, etc) and the GraphQL Foundation oversees everything
else (legal, budget, events, marketing, etc).

The Governing Board steers the GraphQL Foundation while day-to-day operations
are managed by a team of Linux Foundation staff, community and board members.

## Board

The Governing Board is responsible for overall management of the Foundation.

It is made up of:

- **Appointing Members** (19)
  - members from the 19 founding companies.
- **Elected representatives** (up to 5)
  - for all the companies after the 19 founding companies.
- **Community representatives** (2)
  - have voting rights.
- **The executive director of the Foundation** (1)

Some members have specific roles:

- **Board chair**
  - elected by the Board.
  - can change any time.
  - runs meetings, votes, and sets the agenda.
- **Treasurer**
  - elected by the Board.
  - can change any time.
  - assists in the preparation of budgets for Governing Board approval, monitors
    expenses against the budget and authorizes expenditures approved in the
    budget.

The Board may invite any number of guests to attend meetings without voting
rights.

## Requesting something from the Foundation

If you need something from the Foundation, email `operations@graphql.org`.

## Asking a question to the board

Two TSC representatives may attend the monthly GraphQL Foundation Board Meeting
(without being Foundation members themselves); these are currently Benjie Gillam
and Michael Staib. Board meetings are listed on the
[Foundation calendar](https://calendar.graphql.org). Should you have a topic you
wish to be discussed, please bring it to Benjie and/or Michael.
