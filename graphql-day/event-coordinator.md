title: Coordinating an event
sidebar_position: 3
---

The event coordinator makes sure everything goes smoothly. They don't need to do
everything themselves but just ensure that things are taken care of and/or ping
other people about it.

It's a lot of very small interrupt-driven tasks during ~3 months.

Event coordinators are what makes those events possible.

## Checklist

- CFP
  - Reaching out to potential speakers to fill the CFP
    - DMs work best
    - Local speakers/companies are very welcome
  - Organize a CFP Review
    - This can usually be done in a single shot
  - Publish the Agenda on the website
- Social media
  - "Save the date"
  - "Tickets are available"
  - "Agenda is available"
    - Tweet about each individual talk
  - "Booth setup/photo, join us at GraphQL Day $\{location\}"
  - "Thank you!"
- Booth
  - Have every staff member expense
    [a pink t-shirt](https://graphql-foundation-shop.fourthwall.com/products/ask-me-about-graphql)
    if they don't have one already
  - Pink table cloth
  - Rollups
    - Designs: https://graphql.org/conf/conference-kit/
    - Do not put dates/locations so they can be reused
  - 1xA4 Plexi holder with the schedule
  - 1xA4 Plexi holder with a link to graphql.org and/or raffle
  - Stickers
    - We recommend
      [Sticker Mule die cut](https://www.stickermule.com/products/die-cut-stickers)
    - Regular GraphQL logo: 2" x 2"
    - "Fun" ones
    - Community stickers: gather vendor/community stickeres to showcasse the
      diversity of the ecosystem
    - Do not put dates/locations so they can be reused
  - Cheatsheet
    - Link [here](https://github.com/graphql/community-wg/tree/main/cheatsheet)
    - Print on >160g/m2 A4 paper
  - Flyers: "Why join the foundation"
  - GraphQL demo
  - Plushies, SWAG, etc...
