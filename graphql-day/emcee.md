The emcee welcomes attendees in the track and introduces speakers.

## Checklist

- Make sure the speaker is present 10min before their talk
- A/V test
- In case there needs to be a live demo, make sure to have hands-free mics
- Have water ready for the speaker
- Remind the speaker 5min/1min before the end of their talk (some phone apps
  display a large text, that can work)
- Double check pronunciation
- Announce breaks
- Thank the organizers
